U1 = bottom. Nothing on the cabinet top. Diagram: `rack.drawio.svg`.

Proxmox tower + CST1500SUC share a rolling platform beside the rack (locking casters). Interior ~90 lb; cabinet ~195 lb loaded.

U18     CBS350

U13–16  4U vented shelf — Denon (fixed 4-post, ≥16–18″ deep; not sliding)
U11–12  2U vented shelf — NCx500 (desktop case, no ears)

U6–7    2U shelf — S33
U4–5    reserved — NCx252MP
U3      blank / airflow
U1–2    optional shallow drawer (PDU is behind U1)

Denon sits high for heat (top exhaust) and short HDMI/pre-out runs.

PS5 must lie flat (vertical is 8–9U). Width is tight on a 19″ shelf — pick: ≥22″ deep shelf, rotate PS5 90°, or split to 4U.

Install bottom-up. Power: `power.md`.
